Canvas Middleware

Flinders University · Integration platform

Canvas on its own doesn't know which students belong in which course, how co-taught or merged offerings should combine, or which cohorts to enrol automatically. The middleware holds that knowledge. It ingests course, enrolment and identity data from the university's student, finance and identity systems, applies the university's rules, and keeps Canvas in sync.

I wrote most of the codebase and worked through the major design decisions with the team.
